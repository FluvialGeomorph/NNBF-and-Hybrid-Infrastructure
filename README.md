# NNBF-and-Hybrid-Infrastructure

Natural and Nature-Based Features (NNBF) are landscape features that combine engineering functions with economic, environmental, and social benefits. The focus is on integrating natural processes into design concepts to enhance the resiliency and ecological benefits of water-resource projects. Traditional channel stabilization techniques and design involve activities like bank stabilization, grade control, floodplain reconnection, or re-meandering. Other concepts provide additional benefits for water quality, aquatic and terrestrial habitat, and aesthetics. 

The US Army Corps of Engineers is updating river engineering practices by incorporating NNBF and natural infrastructure strategies into the design of stable channels. The stabilization and restoration design approach updates previous principles, includes new technological advances, and incorporates NNBF concepts. This approach is flexible and can be adjusted to a wide range of project types.
